Session-RPE training load is a simple session score: duration of the session multiplied by the athlete's rating of perceived exertion (RPE) for that session. Foster and colleagues showed this "session-RPE" product tracks internal load without needing a heart-rate strap, which is why AthleteIQ can score Tier 3 (manual) athletes on the same load scale as wearable users.

In this product, duration is minutes and RPE is the 1–10 session rating the athlete logs. Rest days store duration and RPE as 0, so that day's training load is 0 rather than missing. Acute and chronic loads, ACWR, and the Fitness–Fatigue index all consume this same session-RPE number — they are not fed separate data streams.

Session-RPE is a perception-based estimate, not a lab measurement. Two athletes can produce different loads for the same workout if they rate effort differently. Use it as a consistent within-athlete signal, not as a cross-athlete ranking of who "trained harder."
